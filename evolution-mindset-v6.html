Frequently asked questions

How long does delivery take?

Most orders arrive in 3–6 working days across India. Metro cities are often faster. You will get tracking details by email once your order ships.

Is delivery free?

Yes. Delivery is free on orders above ₹499. Below that, a flat shipping fee of ₹49 applies.

What is your return policy?

You can return a book within 7 days of delivery if it is damaged, defective or not the title you ordered. Items must be unused and in original packaging.

Which payment methods do you accept?

UPI, debit and credit cards, net banking, popular wallets and cash on delivery (select pin codes). All payments are encrypted.

Are the books original and new?

Every book is a new, original copy sourced directly from publishers and authorised distributors.

Can I order in bulk or gift books?

Yes. We offer gift wrapping and discounts on orders of 10 or more copies. Use the contact form below and we will get back to you within a day.

How do I track my order?

Log in and open My Orders from your profile menu, or use the tracking link in your shipping confirmation email.
